## The result memo keyed a box on the whole slot environment and on `suppress_fad`, so a definition read inside a `~` body was propagated again, and every clocked block in it allocated once more; the key is now the environment restricted to the box's free slots, and the flag only for a box holding a `fad`

Commit date: 2026-09-21

Source commit: this commit

Found by faust-diff-demo, the X-ray demonstrator of faust-diff-ts808's
learner: a TS-808 clipping stage identified while it runs, two descents
in parallel, a leader and a scout. The program cost 207 ms per second of
audio at 48 kHz where two independent descents cost 122 and one 55, and
the prepared signal DAG held 28 clocked blocks where the program has 12:
the solver's two nested `ondemand` blocks, the step block of every
descent, the twin of every block a `fad` augments. Reduced to four lines:

```
t = (clk, x) : ondemand(exp);
process = t + ((\(r).(t + 0.5 * r)) ~ _);
```

two blocks for one definition, `exp` twice in the C++. The result memo
(`propagate/result_memo.rs`) keys a call on `(box, slot environment, UI
path, mode, inputs)`, as C++ `propagate` does; a `~` whose left side is an
unapplied function is a `boxSymbolic`, its body is propagated with the
slot bound, and the closed definition `t` reached there had a key its
first propagation did not. For a plain signal the second propagation
rebuilds the same hash-consed tree; a clocked wrapper allocates a domain
per miss (`make_clock_env`), so the two propagations are two blocks that
both run, and the twin a `fad` makes of each doubles again. Every
`optimizers.lib` descent has this shape (`loop ~ (_, ...)`, `loop`
unapplied), so a loss reading a clocked model or a clocked target, and
the program reading the same outside the descent, paid one block per
reading. Three parts:

1. `flat::free_slots` computes, per flat box and memoised, the sorted
   list of its free slots: the `Slot` nodes below it that no `Symbolic`
   below it binds. `SlotEnv::restricted_id(slots)` interns the chain of
   their current bindings from the empty environment, in that order: a
   canonical id that two environments share when they bind those slots
   to the same signals, whatever else they bind. `propagate` keys the
   memo on it, the empty id for a closed box. A first version answered
   "mentions a slot" and kept every `Symbolic` on the full key; that
   left `ondemand(\(u).(exp(u) - 1.0))`, whose binder binds the only slot
   it mentions, and every `f ~ g` body, on the old behaviour. A program
   written as `process(xin, tin) = ...` mentions its two inputs' slots
   everywhere, so "closed" alone would have changed nothing for it: the
   restriction is what makes the key reusable under the lifted
   environment of a nested `~`, where `Input(0)` lifted is `Input(0)`.
2. `suppress_fad`, the flag of `RecFadMode::ExpandAfterRec`, changes the
   propagation of a `ForwardAD` box alone; it leaves the key of a box
   that contains none (`contains_forward_ad`). The target a loss reads
   inside a recursion, propagated under the flag, and the same target
   read outside were two propagations.
3. A `Rec` that contains a `fad` is memoised from its first call, like
   the identity-allocating nodes: its expansion after the group runs the
   forward-AD transform, whose twins are domains allocated outside the
   blocks' own memo entries, so a second propagation of the recursion
   during the warm-up expanded it again. That second propagation came
   from the output model of the X-ray, a clocked block written as
   `f ~ g` (the skip test of `ts808.lib`'s `stage_repeat_live`) whose
   body inlines the descent's box: a second twin of the model and a
   second step block for one learner.

Measured with `faustprobe --time` at 48 kHz, ten seconds of a sine, clocked
blocks counted in `--dump-sig-dag-prepared`:

| program | blocks before | after | ms per second of audio before | after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ts808_online.dsp` (faust-diff-ts808), 3 lanes | 8 | 5 | 47 | 29 |
| `ts808_xray.dsp` (faust-diff-demo), 5 lanes | 8 | 5 | 55 | 32 |
| two independent descents, no adoption | 18 | 12 | 122 | 70 |
| `ts808_xray_pair.dsp`, leader and scout | 28 | 15 | 207 | 93 |

Samples are unchanged: the learners' trajectories to the printed
precision, the four-line witness to the reference frames of the new test.
The gain is not the demo's alone: any descent whose loss reads a clocked
model and whose program reads that model too, that is any online learner
of `optimizers.lib` with a clocked block in it, paid it.

What remains, recorded in the pair: 15 blocks for 12. The leader's
descent depends on the fed-back adoption signal, a projection of the
enclosing recursion's group, and is read at two binder depths, at the
body's level and inside a nested `~`. Under the nested binder the
projection is lifted by one de Bruijn level, so the bindings differ, the
restricted key differs, and the box is propagated once more: two
spellings of one term, which the symbolic conversion merges for plain
signals but not for clocked blocks, whose domains are distinct by
construction. Merging them would take a pass over the prepared DAG that
identifies blocks equal modulo their own domain token; the C++ reference
has the same duplication by the same key. Left open. The smallest
witness is `notes/witnesses/solver_reset.dsp` of faust-diff-demo, fifteen
lines, a two-parameter descent through the TS-808 solver inside a `~`
whose fed-back flag is the descent's reset: 10 clocked blocks after this
change against 7 for the same program with a button as reset (14 and 14
before it).

The compile-budget gate could not run on the machine of this change:
its calibration program, `karplus.dsp`, compiles in 3 ms there, under
the 4 ms floor the ratio needs, on the unchanged tree as well.

Tests: `closed_clocked_box_is_one_block_across_slot_environments` and
`free_slots_stop_at_the_binder` in `propagate`, `restricted_id_names_only_the_slots_asked`
in `context_id`, and `closed_box_memo.rs` in the compiler crate: the
four-line witness emits one `exp(` and renders the frames it rendered
before, and a library-free program of the X-ray's shape, `process(xin,
tin)`, a descent with a clocked model under `fad`, and an output model
fed by the descent inside a `~` body, emits two `exp(` where it emitted
more.

The witnesses that found it had first said something else: a descent
whose reset depends on the enclosing recursion was "instantiated three
times", against a control whose reset was a button. The control's error
model was dead code and eliminated; with the flag kept alive the two
programs are identical. The note in faust-diff-demo records the
correction. The `faustprobe --quiet` switch also silences the `--every`
rows, which the `make online` targets of two projects had not noticed.
