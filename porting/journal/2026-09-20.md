# 2026-09-20

## The reverse sweep of `rad` replayed a recursion held by the operand of `exp`, `sqrt` or `abs`, advancing the forward state once more per block; the sweep now reads tapes only, and refuses what it cannot

Commit date: 2026-09-20

Source commit: this commit

Found by faust-diff-amp, the DDSP guitar amp of Yeh et al. (2024) ported
next to faust-rs: the loss lane of `rad(loss, params)` over a
Wiener-Hammerstein block, a gated recurrent unit between two biquad
cascades, differed between block sizes from the first frame after a
block boundary (0.085 on a loss of 0.01 at block 1 or 64, identical when
one block covered the render), while the `fad` program with the same
loss was the same at every block size. A primal must not depend on the
block. Reduced with `faustprobe --out` at block 1 and block 4096 over
twelve witnesses: a recursion under `rad` whose body holds `exp`,
`exp10` or `sqrt` of an operand that is not stateless (here a phasor:
`x + exp(-x*x) * hp` with `x = sin(2 pi phasor)`), or `abs` of one,
while `tanh`, `sin`, `log`, `pow`, a division and plain products are
exact. The C++ emitted for the witness showed the cause in the reverse
loop: the phasor's whole body lowered again, `fRec1 = fRecCur1`
included, its value used by nothing. `propagate_bra_unary_math_adj`
loaded the operand for every rule, and `collect_tape_needed_values`
tapes the *output* of `exp`, `exp10` and `sqrt` (their derivative is a
multiple of it), not the operand; `load_bra_fwd_value`, finding no tape,
fell back to `lower_signal`, which in the reverse loop emits the
operand's computation, and the state update of any recursion in it. The
dead value was eliminated; the update was not; the forward state
advanced twice per sample of the block. `abs` had the mirror case: its
rule reads the operand (taped) and its own output (not taped, so
lowered again, recursion included).

The fix: for `exp`, `exp10` and `sqrt` the sweep loads the taped output
alone; for `abs` it rebuilds `|x|` from the taped operand. And
`load_bra_fwd_value` no longer falls back silently: a value that is
neither taped nor trivially re-evaluable (a leaf or a stateless
combinator of leaves, the same predicate the tape analysis uses) is
refused with `FRS-SFIR-0004` naming the signal, so a rule and the tape
analysis that disagree on an operand are an error, not a drifting
primal. The causal placement (the sweep inside the forward loop) keeps
lowering, as before, since there the horizon is the current sample.
Tests `a_rad_whose_exp_operand_holds_a_recursion_keeps_the_primal_across_blocks`
and `..._abs_...` in `rad_runtime.rs`: two blocks of 64 must give the
samples 64..128 of one block of 128 (before the fix the second block was
off by 0.02 on 0.34); with the operand lowered again as a mutation the
first fails at frame 64, and with the output loaded again for `abs` the
guard refuses the program. The guard also caught `debug_softclip_gradient_values`
(`abs(0.0 * float(lcg))`) before the `abs` rule was corrected: the
same defect on a noise source, silent until then.

What the amp project measured with the defect in place is remeasured
there: a truncation table that attributed to the block horizon what was
the drifting primal, and a calibration of 209 parameters whose loss was
that of a model no longer the program's after the first block. The
one-block gradients against finite differences were right all along (no
boundary is crossed), which is why the defect hid behind them.

## A slider's initial value reached a `-double` program as its `f32` rounding

Commit date: 2026-09-20

Source commit: this commit

Also found by faust-diff-amp: an online descent rendered by `faustprobe`
followed one trajectory with its learning-rate slider at its default and
another with `--set lr=0.02`, the default's own value. The runtime
descriptor of `cranelift-ffi` kept `control_defaults` as `f32`
(`let init = init as f32` before both the UI item and the default), and
`apply_control_defaults` widened that rounding into the `f64` zone:
`hslider("lr", 0.02, ...)` started at 0.019999999552965164, a `--set`
delivered 0.02. A descent whose step is that value, through a saturating
chain with bounded parameters, diverged from the other in a few hundred
steps. The defaults are now kept at the FIR's precision (`f64`) and
narrowed only for an `f32` zone; the UI item's fields stay `f32`, the
width of the host interface. Test
`a_slider_starts_from_the_double_its_source_wrote` in
`crates/cranelift-ffi/tests/host_loop_probe.rs`: `--double` on
`hslider("lr", 0.02, 0, 1, 0.0001)` prints `0.02`, and `--set lr=0.02`
prints the same bits.
