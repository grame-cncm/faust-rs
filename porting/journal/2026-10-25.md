## Incremental normalization of a sum extended by new terms

Commit date: 2026-10-25

Source commit: this commit

Phase 2 of `porting/long-fir-iir-compile-cost-plan-2026-10-25-en.md`.
`simplify` normalized every partial sum of a `:>` chain in full, and so does
the C++ reference: each step decomposed the sum, searched it for a divisor
and folded it back, O(k) per prefix, O(N²) per sum. `normalize::sum_chain`
keeps, for each sum it produced, the state that built it: its terms, the
pending ones, the bucket folds before and after them, the divisor index and
the greatest signature. A parent `T ± y` extends that state.

The result must be identical, down to the order in which arena nodes are
created, because `SigId` order drives the term orders. The fast path
therefore runs the same primitives as the full path, in the same order, and
skips only work whose nodes already exist. It falls back to the full path
from scratch when any of these fails:

- a pending term of `T` must decompose back to the same tree;
- every new term must be appended after the last signature in map order;
- no new term may merge with an existing one;
- every new gcd candidate must have complexity 0.

`Aterm::normalized_tree` is now `SumFolds::push` then `SumFolds::combine`,
the same operations in the same order.

|---|---|---|
| `fi.fir`, 4096 taps, plain | 5.51 → 0.049 s | 6.35 → 0.92 s |
| `fi.iir`, order 128, under `fad` | 3.53 → 1.19 s | — |

The `fi.tf2` cascade under `fad` is unchanged (64 sections: 5 minutes); its
cause is not this one.

`docs/fad-rad-synthesis-{en,fr}.md` §14 is re-measured and corrected: the
FIR cost was never the derivative, whose lanes are one node each.

Validation:

- **Debug-build checker.** It recomputes every fast-path result by the full
  path, which must return the same node and create none. It was active over
  the whole debug workspace suite: 3,435 tests, 0 failures.
- **`sum_chain` unit tests.** They cover:
  - chains joined left, right and alternating;
  - a shared factor;
  - an insertion before the end;
  - a sum used twice;
  - two mutations, a dropped term and an ignored common factor, which the
    checker rejects.
- **Codegen-identity differential.** All 632 `.dsp` of `tests/` and
  `examples/` were compiled in scalar, `-vec` and `-double` modes by the
  base `22999fe5` and the new binary. The 1,896 compilations are identical
  in C++, exit status and stderr.
- **Compile-budget basket, by hand.** Best of five: new/base 0.96 to 1.02.

Registry: `porting/MEMOIZATION.md` §2.29.

## Long FIR and IIR compile cost: analysis, and one de Bruijn check memo per AD output list

Commit date: 2026-10-25

Source commit: this commit

`docs/fad-rad-synthesis-en.md` §14 attributes the `fad` compile cost of a long
FIR or IIR to the derivative. Measured again with `--compilation-time`
(`porting/long-fir-iir-compile-cost-plan-2026-10-25-en.md` §1), it is mostly
not the derivative:

- the plain `fi.fir` of 4096 taps already takes 6.35 s, 5.5 s of it in
  `fir-prepare-normalize`: every partial sum of the `:>` chain is
  normalized in full, O(N²) per sum. The pinned C++ reference takes 12.5 s
  at 1024 taps and times out at 4096;
- `fad` over `fi.iir` of order 128 spends 1.59 s in propagation, cubic in
  the order. The de Bruijn coherence check of the AD outputs gave each
  output a fresh memo, so the DAG the tangents share was walked once per
  output.

The plan has three phases: this check (Phase 1), an incremental
normalization of a sum extended by new terms (Phase 2, output-identical by
construction), and sparse tangent bundles (Phase 3, deferred).

Phase 1:

- `tlib::check_de_bruijn_coherence_with_memo` is the counterpart of
  `de_bruijn_aperture_with_memo`, and one new public item.
- `propagate::forward_ad::check_ad_outputs_coherent` checks the output list
  of `fad` and of `rad` with one aperture memo and one coherence memo.

The coherence memo records successes only, so the first error and its root
are unchanged. Visits went from 26.1 M to 200 k on the order-128 IIR, and
from 6.3 M to 7 k on a 1024-tap FIR under `rad`. Propagation of the
order-128 IIR fell from 1.59 s to 0.072 s. Registry: `porting/MEMOIZATION.md`
§2.28.

Gates:

- passed: fmt, clippy, the `tlib` and `propagate` tests, and the AD suites
  of `compiler`;
- `code-graphs` regenerated for the new item;
- `compile-budget-check` still cannot measure on this machine (3 ms
  calibration, 4 ms floor).
