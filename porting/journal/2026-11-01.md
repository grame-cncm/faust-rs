## diagnostics: name the negation only where it connects the widths

Commit date: 2026-11-01

Source commit: this commit

The evaluation in `faustprobe-diagnostics-eval` found two defects in the guidance of a failing
composition, the same mistake seen from both sides.

`_ : max(-(x))` still got ``A <: B` would split...``, since only `+ - * /` were looked at. The
program it gives compiles and is wrong: `_ <: max(-(x))` differs from `max(0 - x)` from frame 59 of
a sine. `min`, `pow`, `fmod`, `atan2`, `remainder`, `%`, `&`, `<`, `@`, `attach`, `enable`, `control`,
`prefix`, `select2` and `select3` did the same, and the arguments were read in two places only.
Any primitive now counts, through `prim_infix_symbol` and `prim_readable_name`, in any place of a
tree of `Par`.

Extending that search as it stood took a right `<:` away. `source : a, b :> _`, with `source` an
`ol.lf_imptrain`, got the note about `-(x)` for a `_ - _'` deep in the library, and lost the hint
that restores the program. The search read the whole of both operands, and a section far from the
mismatch explains nothing. So it counts only the sections that reach the inputs of the right
operand, and the note needs a negation to connect the widths under the rule of the operator that
failed. Otherwise the hint stands: `_ : (max(-(x)), _)` keeps its `<:`, and so does
`_ : select2(-(x))`, where `0 - x` leaves two inputs.

`-(x)` and `_ - x` are one box after evaluation, so `_ : *(_ - 1)` still gets the note. `f(-(x))`
on a function of two parameters is a partial application, not this, and keeps its hint.

Of the 255 arity mutants, none changes its hint or note against the previous commit, and applying
the hint to the 140 that have one gives the same 39 restored, 37 compiling with other samples and
54 refused. Simulated compiles stay at 3.27. `first_error_diff` finds 2 differences of 2991, both
syntax errors of a `quote` mutant, which never reach this code: with one binary, `noise` flips
between two messages from run to run, and `carre_volterra` moved its count of errors under load.
The tests fail with the width condition removed and with the search
back on `+ - * /`.

## fad: differentiate a block-mode rad, and give each sweep its own carries

Commit date: 2026-11-01

Source commit: this commit

`fad(rad(x', x), x)` failed in signal preparation with `projection 12 does not target symbolic
recursion`: `fad` had no rule for `SigBlockReverseAD`, gave the carrier a zero tangent and built
`Proj(i, 0.0)`. The tests of Phase B1 stopped at `compile_source_to_signals`, where the shape
looks fine.

`Proj(slot, BlockReverseAD)` now has a forward rule (`transform_block_reverse_proj` in
`crates/propagate/src/forward_ad.rs`). A primal slot takes the tangent of its body. A gradient
slot exchanges the derivatives, `d/dz (dJ/ds) = d/ds (dJ/dz)`, so it is the gradient of a second
carrier over the tangent of the body, one per `fad` seed. The truncation is the original's: the
state entering a block is a constant for the sweep, and its tangent, carried across `compute()`
calls, is read by the tangent body. Outputs are checked sample by sample against central finite
differences over three blocks, at `opt_level` 0 and max, on a delay, a recursion, a driven
recursion, a two-seed Hessian and a `fad` seed distinct from the `rad` seed; the probe also gives
the same values under `--vec`, `--double` and `--scheduling-strategy 1`.

The exchange reads the `rad` seed as an independent variable, which fails when its tangent is an
expression: the second sweep walks it, `exp(z)` for a seed `s = exp(z)` being the seed itself, and
the tangent slot of a recursion reading the seed's previous sample. Such a seed is refused in
propagation (`FRS-PROP-0005`, `FadOfRadWithDependentSeed`), located on the `fad`. This includes
`fad` around an adaptive loop whose gradient is a block sweep; it failed before too, with the
internal message. Recorded in DIFF-SRC-002 and DIFF-GAP-006.

Validating this found a wrong result that needs no `fad`: `delay1_carry_vars` and
`delay_array_carry_vars` were keyed by the delay node, the `_group` argument unused, and
hash-consing shares a delay between carriers, so two sweeps stored into one carry and the last
store won: `rad(s * s, a), rad(s * b, a)` over `s = a'` gave the first the second's gradient. Both
are now keyed by `(group, node)`.

The fixture is renamed `fad_rad_delay1_block.dsp`, the `err_` prefix being the negative corpus.
`check_json_sfir_family_failure_is_clean` moves to `err_rad_writable_table_block.dsp`, and the
writable-table refusal of the sweep now carries its signal, hence a source label. Goldens:
`cargo run -p xtask -- golden-gen-rust`, those three entries only (`bytes`, `fnv1a64`, no C++
reference involved). Historical reports that name the old fixture are left as they are.

## diagnostics: name the operator a composition mismatch may have wanted

Commit date: 2026-11-01

Source commit: this commit

In the blind repairs of `faustprobe-diagnostics-eval`, a mismatch of `:` was often a `<:` or
a `:>` written `:`, and agents had to guess it from the widths, sometimes wrongly. A mismatch
whose widths divide now says so: ``A <: B` would split the 1 output(s) of A over the 2 inputs
of B`. It is a help line, shown at `standard` and first at `concise`, and nothing is said when
neither operator fits. No measure of this evaluation sees it, since none counts what a help
line changes.

Three other texts were looked at and left. `RBEVN` in a table operand and `% by 0` are built
in `crates/sigtype/src/rules.rs`, which has work in progress. `% by 0` is the wording of C++.
`projection 12 does not target symbolic recursion` is no wording problem: `fad(rad(x', x), x)`,
the fixture `err_fad_rad_temporal.dsp`, reaches signals and then fails in signal preparation.

## eval: report the other undefined symbols of a program

Commit date: 2026-11-01

Source commit: this commit

`process = ax + bx + c;` gave one error, and two more compilations to find the rest.
Each undefined symbol the evaluator reaches now gets its diagnostic.

The plan was to record a failed lookup and go on in the same run. Reading the evaluator
ruled that out: it tries things and drops their error (`a2sb(...).ok()` on arguments, a
constant tried in `iteration.rs`), so a symbol met there does not fail a program today, and
recording it would refuse programs that compile. Instead the ordinary run stands. When it
fails on an undefined symbol, a further run with its own `LoopDetector` reads each one as `_`
and records it. Which programs are accepted and their first error cannot change, since the
code that decides them is unchanged. What the further run fails on is dropped, since the `_`
may have caused it, and a symbol found this way may lie in code the repaired program does not
reach, which a note says.

The cost is on the error path: at most twice the first run, at least 1 s, polled with the
cancel flag, and at most 8 errors. `a_run_past_its_budget_stops` fails with the check removed.
The Faust scope rules are not restated anywhere.

On the 2991 mutants, the previous binary and this one agree on every verdict and every first
error. Simulated compiles go from 3.30 to 3.27, little because two wrong names rarely meet
in that mix. On the 20 programs of the blind repairs, which hold two typos each, a prototype
found both at once in 17 of 19. The first estimate, a sixth of the attempts, was wrong: it is
about 7 % there, and the blind repairs were not run again. Analysis and plan in
`porting/multi-error-semantic-diagnostics-analysis-and-plan-2026-11-01-en.md`.

`compile-budget-check` cannot run on this Mac. The success path gains a `match` on the result
of evaluation and an `Instant::now`.

## diagnostics: keep to the facts of the failure at the standard level

Commit date: 2026-11-01

Source commit: this commit

What remained of the text the evaluation in `faustprobe-diagnostics-eval` measured
restated the header or held for every failure of a kind. On an undefined symbol,
`cause`, `rule` and `computed` said in three lines that the symbol is undefined, and
three help lines said to define it.

`cause:` and `rule:` notes, and `general:` and `template:` help, move to `debug`.
`Standard` keeps what depends on this failure: `computed` notes with values,
`suggested target`, `did you mean`, specific help and fixes. `Concise` shows the
first specific help, so each level stays a superset of the one below, and an
undefined symbol has none there. The JSON report keeps all of it.

The filter is on these prefixes, in `diagnostics_human.rs`, next to the one for
`node_id=`. A typed tier would have meant changing a hundred producers for a
distinction `normalize_note_order` already reads from the same prefixes. Generic help took the
prefix `general:`. Three `computed:` notes that repeated their header became `cause:`
(the scope of a symbol, `2 == 3 -> false`, the tuple of a `case`), and so did
`provided=2, expected_max=1`, the reason of a modulation circuit, and `first bound to
node N`, the last place a node number was shown.

`iteration count is not an int node: 309` printed a node number too, and now says
`iteration count is not an integer constant`.

Reading all 37 forms of error the corpus produces at this level, none becomes
unclear: each keeps a header that says what failed, and the location. That is a
reading, not a measure, and the blind repairs of the evaluation have not been run
again on this text.

Median text on the 2754 mutants goes from 491 to 454 characters, 71 for C++. The
median hides the two families: syntax errors, the majority, do not change, and
semantic ones fall by a third or more, arity from 1119 to 780 and a misspelt name
from 1000 to 604. Lines reported are unchanged, so simulated compiles and lines read
do not move.
