# 2026-09-14

## A `fad`/`rad` seed parameter owns one UI leaf

Commit date: 2026-09-14

Source commit: this commit

A widget referenced from a `fad(…)` or `rad(…)` seed is unified into one control
across group contexts (`ad_seed_references_unify_to_one_control`, 2026-06):
the seed is walked without group context, the body reads the widget inside its
groups, and both references must be the same differentiation parameter. The
unification was right; its UI side was not: `register_control` returned the
alias, but `input_control` placed a second leaf for it at the root. Measured on
the 33-parameter guitar preamp of `faust-diff-ampli` (`guitar_preamp_fad.dsp`):
33 zones for 66 `addHorizontalSlider` calls, every slider under two paths
(`/guitar_preamp_fad//input_stage/input_HP_freq` and
`/guitar_preamp_fad/input_HP_freq`), the same `varname` under two addresses in
the JSON, every suffix ambiguous for `faustprobe --set` and `--train`, and an
absolute-path label (`hslider("/top/a/g", …)`) rejected by `faustprobe` as a
duplicated control path.
Without AD, `faust-rs` matches the C++ compiler: a widget under two group paths
is two controls (`replicated_widgets_wire_distinct_dsp_fields`).

**Fix** (`crates/propagate/src/ui_build.rs`). `register_control` now returns
whether it created the control; `input_control`, `output_control` and
`soundfile` place a leaf only then, and report it, so the widget arms' `has_ui`
means an actual placement. An aliased seed re-reference keeps the single leaf of
its body occurrence, at the body's group path; a seed the body never reads is
still placed once, by the seed walk, at the root, with a zero tangent. The
signal side (`control_ids`, seed identity) is unchanged.

**Tests** (`crates/compiler/src/tests.rs`):
`ad_seed_reference_is_placed_once_at_its_body_group_path` (fad, rad, the seed
written inside the group, the absolute-path label: one leaf, one field, the leaf
inside the body's group) and `ad_seed_the_body_never_reads_keeps_its_own_ui_leaf`.
The golden snapshots hash the corpus sources, not the generated code, so the
three `fad_recursive_deep_*.dsp` fixtures (seed read inside `vgroup("fb", …)`)
change their generated UI without a golden refresh.

**Docs.** `docs/fad-note-en.md` §2.1 states the rule; the registry entry
DIFF-SRC-001 carries it as adapted behavior shared with `rad`.

**Impact for hosts.** One path per seed parameter. A host that addressed a seed
by its root path (the seed's context-free path) must use the body's group path;
`faustprobe` suffix fragments now resolve. On the preamp the 34 output lanes
(primal and tangents) are bit-identical before and after.

**Gates.** fmt, clippy, the five structure gates, golden-check and the crate
tests pass; `compile-budget-check` could not produce a verdict on the M5 used
here (the calibration DSP runs in 3 ms, under the 4 ms floor).
