## fad beside the plain copy of a block: the augmented twin gets a clock domain of its own

Commit date: 2026-09-19

Source commit: this commit

The defect recorded earlier today as open, then reduced (the shell of
`repeat` on a recursive input and its delay, the input driven by three
library oscillators, consumed both plainly and through `fad` in one
program: the `fad` copy's lane a constant), is understood and fixed. The
C++ emitted for the failing program showed both outer blocks under the
same domain suffix, `_d0`, reading one delay line `iVec1397_d0` for their
first-iteration test; the program with one oscillator, which passed, had
them under `_d0` and `_d2`. The cause is in `forward_ad.rs`: the augmented
twin of a block kept the original's clock-env token, `Clocked(c, u)' =
Clocked(c, u')`, and the block's inputs and clock, wrapped in
`Clocked(c, ·)` but reached by no seed, were passed through untouched by
the aperture rule, so the twin was a second block of the *same* domain,
sharing its local-time delays and holds; run after the original in the
same sample, it read a first-iteration test already consumed and never
fired. Whether the two blocks ended up in one domain depended on what
else the transform rebuilt, which is why one oscillator hid it.

The fix, in `augment_block`: the twin gets a domain of its own, allocated
in the propagation run's `ClockDomainTable` as a twin of the original's
(same kind, the parent mapped through the twins already made for a nested
block), and a fresh token; the `Clocked` arm renames the original's token
to the twin's; and a subtree carrying a token being renamed is rebuilt
even when no seed reaches it (`needs_rebuild`, on a memo of the tokens a
subtree contains), so that the twin's inputs, clock and state are its own.
`generate_fad_signals_multi` takes the table; the engine passes
`ctx.clock_domains`. The witness is now the regular test
`fad_and_plain_copies_of_a_repeat_shell_on_a_recursion_and_its_delay_agree`
(on a 64 MB stack: the library import in a debug build), which failed with
"the fad primal lane is constant" on the code before the fix and passes
after; a first version of the fix without `needs_rebuild` failed six
fad-around-block tests with `FRS-SFIR-0008` (the twin's lanes wrapped
inputs still annotated with the original's domain), which is the check
that the rename has to reach the untouched subtrees. Green: the ondemand
pipeline (43), propagate (113), `fad_recursive_runtime`, `rad_runtime`,
`optimizers_lib`, `ddsp_examples`, `interleave_fft`, the C++ clocked
differential, `clocked_shared_box_one_domain`,
`clocked_emission_structure`, `block_reverse_ad`; `golden-check` (205),
`structure-check`, `code-graphs --check`, the `faustprobe` output
recordings over the whole corpus; clippy and fmt. On Cranelift the two
project witnesses (the reduced one and the full TS-808 stage, plain and
through `fad` in one program) now give identical lanes to the bit, and the
project's twelve checks pass unchanged.

Two lessons kept. A stale release binary (`impulse-runner`, not rebuilt
after the morning's fix) faked an interpreter-only defect for an hour:
rebuild every release binary after a compiler change, the skill says so.
And a witness reduced by hand must be checked to fail: the library-free
version of this one, with an explicit phasor instead of `os.osc`, passed
on the unfixed compiler and would have been a test of nothing.

## fad through a clocked block whose flag feeds its own clock: the augmented block kept the untransformed clock

Commit date: 2026-09-19

Source commit: this commit

Found from the sibling project `faust-diff` (the TS-808 overdrive stage of
Esqueda, Kuznetsov and Parker, DAFx 2021, solved on the library form of
`repeat`): the loss of the calibration program at the hidden stage's own
values was 3e-2 instead of 0, and `fad`'s primal of the `repeat` block
differed from the plain block by 1.2e-2. The smallest witness is a counting
body: an integer `ondemand` of clock 5 around a boolean `ondemand` whose
clock is "first iteration of the sample, or the flag the body held at the
previous iteration" (the shell of `repeat`), the body a counter whose
increment depends on the seed times zero. Plain, the counter reads 5, 10,
15 per sample; under `fad`, 1, 2, 3: one iteration per sample. With the
seed kept out of the body the count was right, which pointed at
`augment_block` (`forward_ad.rs`): it pushed the block's clock
*untransformed*, so an augmented inner block kept a clock that read the
flag of its pre-transform twin through the original recursion group,
while the consumers read the augmented block; the held flag it saw was
never the one it produced, and the loop stopped after the first
iteration. The 2026-09-18 test of the nested-flag shape had a stateless
body, which reads the same after one iteration or three. The fix is one
line: the augmented block gets the *transformed* clock's primal (a clock is
opaque to the derivative, but it is a signal of the transformed program,
and the rebuilt recursion is what it must read). The test
`fad_around_nested_block_runs_the_iterations_its_flag_asks_for` counts 5,
10, 15 and 3, 8, 13 for a flag always 1 and a flag `c % 5 != 3`; it
failed with "reads 1 where 5 iterations were asked" before the fix (the
reverting mutation). The ondemand pipeline (42), the propagate tests
(113), `fad_recursive_runtime`, `rad_runtime`, `optimizers_lib`,
`ddsp_examples`, `interleave_fft` and the C++ clocked differential are
green; clippy and fmt clean. On the project's program the loss at the
hidden values is now 4e-11.

## `repeat`: the primitive renamed, and a note on its semantics and on the three wrappers as its derived forms

Commit date: 2026-09-19

Source commit: this commit

Asked to rename `iterate` to `repeat` in the plan of 2026-09-18, then to
write a synthesis note in `docs/`, in French and in English, on the new
primitive: its precise semantics, how `ondemand`, `upsampling` and
`downsampling` are built on it, the emblematic uses, and the motivation for
having `repeat` as such for the loops that run to an end condition; no
implementation. The rename touches the keyword and the identifiers only
(46 occurrences; the noun "the iterate", the current value of a solver,
stays), the file is renamed with it and its two references updated, and
decision D1 now records the name as taken and the polarity as open, the
idiom `repeat … until` arguing for a stop flag where the library form of
§1.5 implements a continue flag. The note, `docs/repeat-note-{en,fr}.md`:
the loops Faust has (`par`/`seq` at compile time, `~` one step per sample,
integer `ondemand` a count decided outside) and the class defined by an
"until" (root finding to a tolerance, iterative refinement of a per-sample
linear system, adaptive step control, backtracking line search, a search
stopping at the first acceptable candidate, SPSA in one sample); what the
two-layer form costs (4 to 12 % of the solver, two guards and a held flag
in place of a loop, a shape to recognise each time) and what a name buys
(a contract, the bound and the flag's evaluation point as rules, one rule
for `fad`, the three wrappers as derived forms). The semantics on one page
and as equations: outer time and local time counting executed iterations,
inputs snapshotted at the sample, `k_t = min(n_t, 1 + min{j : F = 0})`,
outputs of the last executed iteration held, the flag after the outputs,
`n = int(H)` with negative clocks as 0, `ma.SR` unchanged. The derived
forms: `ondemand(C) = repeat((C, 1))`, so the two readings of `ondemand`
are one rule; `downsampling` a counter's firing in the outer domain plus
the rate annotation `SR / H`; `upsampling` the zero-stuffing with the
sample on the last iteration plus `SR · H`, the placement forced by
`upsampling(_) = _` and the reason no exit can apply to it; a table of what
each form adds (a clock expression, an input rewrite, a rate annotation,
never a second loop) and ten identities, four of them measured on
2026-09-18. The non-uniform points stated: `ma.SR` as the one domain
property the loop cannot express, the truncation of a real clock, the
unconditional first iteration, the polarity. The uses with the numbers of
the day: the Newton solver in one layer (0.25, 5.3, 6.1, 7.3 ms per second
of audio on the four inputs, equal to the unrolled solver to 1.1e-12, the
tangent −0.240418 against −0.24042), the oversampled `tanh` (30 dB gained),
`K` steps per tick, the grid as a loop, the cases where the exit is only
known inside. The docs index and the "see also" of both `ondemand` notes
point to it; the plan's §8 records the rename.
