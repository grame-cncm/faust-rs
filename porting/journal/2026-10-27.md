# 2026-10-27

## New primitives: analysis and general plan

Commit date: 2026-10-27

Source commit: this commit

Added `porting/new-primitives-analysis-and-plan-2026-10-27-en.md`. It
gathers five candidate primitives, checks each against the code, orders
them, and lists the decisions to take before any starts:

1. `detach`, a stop-gradient (specified in §6.2 of the 2026-09-21
   seed-semantics analysis; faust-diff-demo works around its absence with an
   integer cast that quantizes the value);
2. compiler knowledge of the math functions the libraries declare with
   `ffunction`: one classifier table, then intervals, then derivative rules,
   with no new syntax;
3. `withgrad(F, G)`, a derivative given by the program;
4. `nffunction`;
5. `repeat`.

Nothing is implemented.

Measured on the way, and recorded in the plan (§4.2, §6):

- `fad` over an unrecognized `ffunction` gives a zero tangent with no
  diagnostic (`fad(cbrt(x), x)` is 0 at `x = 8`, for 1/12). `rad` rejects
  it with `FRS-PROP-0001`, whose help points to `fad` as having "the same
  coverage".
- `sigtype` gives every `FFun` the default interval, as C++ does, so
  `_ @ int((1 + tanh(x)) * 100)` is rejected by both compilers. The
  `interval` crate already implements the hyperbolic functions.
- `repeat` collides with `si.repeat` in `signals.lib` (faustlibraries
  `9aa3e8f8`, 2023-10-11). D1 of the `repeat` plan is reopened, with a
  dated note in its §8.
