## faustprobe: `--describe`, `--points`, sweeps of a polyphonic instrument

Commit date: 2026-10-24

Source commit: this commit

The faust-elements sound atlas (about 2000 patches, eight descriptors each)
was computed in node with the page's wasm DSPs, partly because
`faustprobe` could neither sweep a polyphonic instrument (`--sweep` was
refused under `--nvoices`) nor take a list of points rather than a cartesian
product, so that every patch paid a JIT (about 1.2 s for faust-elements).

- `PolyProbe::reset`: every voice and the effect back to their defaults with
  their state cleared, every voice free. `--sweep`, `--reduce` and the new
  options now work under `--nvoices`, each point from a reset instrument with
  the schedule's notes played again; a point renders what the same command
  renders alone with `--set` (tested in both orders).
- `--points FILE`: a sweep read from a CSV file (header of control queries,
  one point per line, `#` comments), checked against the ranges before any
  render like `--sweep`'s values. Refused with `--sweep`, `--train`,
  `--freqresp`.
- `--describe LIST` (`--describe-hop`, `--describe-fft`): loudness, attack,
  `decay[:DB]`, `sustain:A..B`, `centroid[:A..B]`, `width[:A..B]`, defined in
  `probe::describe` (FFI-free) and in the user guide: on the window, times
  from its first frame, the mid for all but `width`, 10 ms envelope frames,
  Hann transforms of 2048 samples. A value that does not exist is `NaN`/`-inf`
  (JSON `null`) with a note, never a number standing for it (a decay not
  reached is not the window's length). Spectral flatness and harmonicity were
  left out: their floor, band and fundamental are the study's choices.
- `--reduce` and `--describe` are computed by one module of the binary
  (`measure.rs`) for the scalar and the polyphonic paths; the scalar render's
  recordings are unchanged.
- Cross-check against `gen_atlas.mjs`: the atlas's first 124 points (its 24
  presets and 100 random patches) rendered with `--points` in single
  precision, voice and body as one program at the atlas's gain, reproduce all
  671 stored values to their four digits (decays not reached excluded); one
  value altered by hand is flagged. 124 renders of two seconds take 4 s with
  one compilation.
- Tests: unit tests of the descriptors on analytic signals and of the points
  parser; `tests/describe_probe.rs` (closed-form values through the binary,
  `--points` against `--sweep`, a polyphonic sweep against single renders,
  refusals); `poly_probe.rs` (a reset instrument renders a fresh one's bits);
  31 recordings in `output_snapshots.rs`, the two `poly_refuses_sweep`/
  `poly_refuses_reduce` recordings replaced by the runs they now are.
  Removing the voices' reset in `PolyProbe::reset` fails the sweep test.
- Registry: DIFF-CLI-011 lists the new options.
- Gates: `cargo fmt`, clippy, `cargo test --workspace --all-targets` (1743
  tests; the one failure of that run was a recording blessed with a stale
  binary, re-blessed after a rebuild, then `cranelift-ffi` rerun alone: 459
  passed) and the five structure checks pass; `code-graphs` regenerated for
  the new public items, and `run_poly` split (`check_writes`) to stay under
  the function threshold.

## Delay amounts: refuse the whole integer range and `hi >= INT_MAX`

Commit date: 2026-10-24

Source commit: this commit

A delay read at an offset that follows an oscillator through `int()`
(`wp @ (int(10 + 80 * lfo) - 1)`, from the faust-elements reverb) has an
amount typed `[INT_MIN - 1, INT_MAX - 1]`: `int()` of an unbounded value
saturates to the `i32` range. C++ refuses it for its negative lower bound;
`check_delay_interval` tolerates a negative lower bound and accepted it,
sizing the line to 2^31 samples (`float fRec1[2147483648]`, an 8 GiB class
that crashes a C++ host allocating it on the stack; `faustprobe` rendered it).

- `sigtype::check_delay_interval` now also refuses an upper bound of
  `INT_MAX` or more (the C++ condition `i.hi() < INT_MAX`, which the port had
  dropped) and an interval spanning the whole integer range
  (`hi - lo >= 2^32 - 1`). The tolerance of a negative lower bound is kept and
  now registered (`DIFF-BEH-026`).
- Tests: four `sigtype` unit tests; two end-to-end tests in
  `crates/compiler/tests/diagnostic_errors.rs`, the reduced case refused with
  `delay-interval` and the same delay clamped compiling without a 2^31 line.
  `prepare_signals_for_fir_keeps_sampling_frequency_in_delay_amounts_after_simplify`
  now bounds its sample rate as `ma.SR` does: `int()` of a bare
  `fSamplingFreq` spans the whole integer range, an amount C++ refuses too.
- Gates: `cargo fmt`, clippy, `cargo test --workspace --all-targets`,
  `golden-check`, the five structure checks and `compile-budget-check` pass
  (the budget check reports six stale baselines, all faster than recorded,
  unrelated to this change).
- Considered and not done: full C++ parity (`lo >= 0`). It refuses `rep_67`,
  which C++ compiles because it checks delays after normalization, where
  `x @ 100 @ (s - 100)` folds; faust-rs does not apply the delay normal form
  at the top-level rate. Porting it changes generated code for many delay
  programs and breaks `BlockReverseAD` on folded delays read on a recursion
  output; left for a separate change.
