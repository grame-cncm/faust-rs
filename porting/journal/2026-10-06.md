## Tutorial: the sliders of an existing program become section 13

Commit date: 2026-10-06

Source commit: this commit

**Why.** §11.5 (reading and measuring the sliders) and §11.6 (learning
them) sat under section 11, "Learning at its own rate: `ondemand`", but
§11.5 uses no `ondemand` at all and §11.6 only through `adaptive_fad`.
Their subject is taking an existing program, not the clock.

**New layout**, in `libraries/optimizers-ddsp-tutorial-{en,fr}.md`:
- section 11 keeps §11.1 to §11.4;
- section 12, "When the start is wrong", is unchanged;
- new section 13, "The sliders of an existing program":
  - a short introduction naming its prerequisites, `fad`/`rad` for 13.1 and
    the clocked loop of §11.2 for 13.2;
  - §13.1 "Reading and measuring the sliders of a program" (the old §11.5);
  - §13.2 "Learning the sliders without rewriting the program" (the old
    §11.6).
- "Where to go next" becomes 14 and "Frequently hit walls" 15.

Placing it after 12 rather than before:
- 12 needs nothing from it;
- its prerequisites all come earlier;
- §13.2's last paragraph can now point to 12. Started at 0 dB, example 15's
  `mid_gain` leaves `mid_freq` without a derivative and the descent
  settles in another basin.

**Text changes.** The opening overview now lists the slider chapter last.
The cross-references move from 11.5/11.6 to 13.1/13.2:
- inside the moved text;
- in the walls table;
- in `libraries/ddsp-examples-{en,fr}.md` §15.

**Tests.** In `crates/cranelift-ffi/tests/tutorial_examples.rs`:
- `s11_5_*` became `s13_1_*` and `s11_6_*` became `s13_2_*`, and they now
  select `program("13.1", …)` and `program("13.2", …)`;
- they moved after the §12 tests under a §13 banner;
- the §12 banner and doc comments, which still said §14, now say §12.
- No program or figure changed: 38/38 pass against faustlibraries
  `master`.

`jos-300-fixes` on this date. With that branch:

  `_saw2ptr_by_precision` case dispatch;
- §5.2 reads 1216 Hz for 1206.

The tests were run with `FAUST_RS_FAUSTLIBRARIES_ROOT` pointing to an
export of `master`. That branch has to be looked at before it merges.
