## Propagation: `enable`/`control` lower to `Control(X*Y, Y != 0)` as in C++

Commit date: 2026-10-06

Source commit: this commit

**Why.** C++ `propagate.cpp` (prim2 case, `gEnableFlag` on, its default)
rewrites `enable(X,Y)` into `sigControl(X*Y, Y != 0)` and `control(X,Y)`
into `sigControl(X, Y != 0)`. Its type rules keep `Control(x, _) = x`
(`sigattributes.cpp`): the product carries the gate's rate into the value.
faust-rs propagated the raw `Enable(x, y)` and lowered it as
`select2(y, x, 0)`. Two divergences followed, both measured with
`-a print_arch.cpp` against faust 2.90.4:
- `0.4536 : enable(checkbox("x"))`: `Enable` has the type of `x`, a
  constant, so the value was hoisted to `instanceConstants` and read the
  checkbox once, at init (0). The output stayed 0 with the checkbox on;
  C++ gives 0.4536.
- `0.8 : enable(hslider("g", 0.5, 0, 1, 0.01))`: C++ gives 0.4 (`X*Y`);
  faust-rs gave 0 (`select2` casts 0.5 to 0).

**Change.** `crates/propagate/src/engine.rs` builds `Control(X*Y, Y != 0)`
for `enable` and `Control(X, Y != 0)` for `control`. Mapping status: 1:1
with C++ under `gEnableFlag` on; the `-es 0` variant (`X*Y` and identity)
is not exposed. The type rules are unchanged (already 1:1). New test
`propagate_enable_and_control_lower_to_cpp_sig_control`
(`crates/propagate/tests/core_api.rs`). Both repros now match C++.

**Known gap (deferred).** The hold semantics of `sigControl` is not ported.
C++ (`ScalarCompiler::conditionAnnotation`, `TempPerm` temporaries in
`compile_scal.cpp`) computes the value's cone under `if (cond)`: its
recursions are frozen and its last value is kept while the condition is
false. faust-rs lowers `Control` as a passthrough in scalar and vector
mode, so a stateful value gated by `enable` is `X*Y`, 0 while the gate is
off, instead of holding. Repro:
`mo.projectedGravity(1.5, 0.08, hslider("a", 0, -1, 1, 0.001)) : enable(checkbox("x"))`
holds 0.4536 after the checkbox is turned off in C++ and gives 0 in
faust-rs. The C++ sources mark enable/control as "slated for removal"
(`compile_scal.cpp`). faustlibraries' only user, `dm.motion_wrapper_demo`,
gates its outputs with `select2` on branch `motion-demo-select2`. A
faithful port would drive both lowerings from the `conditionAnnotation`
port, `vector/analysis/conditions.rs`, which no lowering uses yet.

**Validation.** `cargo fmt --all -- --check` and `cargo clippy --workspace
--all-targets -- -D warnings` clean. `cargo test --workspace --all-targets
--no-fail-fast`: three failures, all present without this change:
`every_modulation_fixture_matches_the_live_reference_compiler`
(`modulation_35_in_recursion.dsp`, frozen from 2.88.1, live compiler
2.90.4) and `s13_1_energy_gradient_rad_against_fad`,
`s13_2_a_rate_per_slider_learns_both` (faustlibraries master now holds
`jos-300-fixes`, see the §5.2 entry below). `compile-budget-check` cannot
run on this machine: the karplus calibration takes 3 ms, under the 4 ms
floor, as the baseline notes record; CI runs it.

## Tutorial §5.2: Lion's jitter checked by means, not by one sample

Commit date: 2026-10-06

Source commit: this commit

**Why.** §5.2 learns a resonant low-pass in log frequency with Lion. Lion
takes fixed-size sign steps, so the frequency does not settle: it jitters
around 1200 Hz. Between samples 8 000 and 12 000 it ranges over 1117 to
1276 Hz, and over the last 10 000 samples its standard deviation is 19 Hz.

The test checked one sample of that jitter, `f` at 10 000, to ±1 Hz.
`tf1s` and `tf2s` are rewritten on faustlibraries `jos-300-fixes`. At a
fixed setting, its `fi.resonlp` agrees with `master` to 1.2e-14, yet the
sample read 1216 instead of 1206. Both versions converge alike: the means
over the last 10 000 samples are 1199.0 and 1201.1. The tutorial text
quoted the same single samples, `(1206, 2.003)` at 10 000 and
`(1233, 2.03)` at 20 000.

**Change.**
- Test `s05_2_log_frequency_with_lion` now checks:
  - the means over the last 10 000 samples, `f` 1199.0 ± 6 and `q`
    1.993 ± 0.02;
  - that no sample after 11 000 strays more than 10 % from `(1200, 2.0)`.
    The worst measured is 6.3 % on `f` and 4.8 % on `q`.
- The text (EN/FR) now says the printed values are single samples of the
  jitter. It gives the means from `--skip 20000 --quiet`, 1199.0 and 1.993,
  and the frequency's peak, 1275.

**Measured with the two faustlibraries versions.** On `master`, 38/38
tutorial tests pass. On `jos-300-fixes`, 36/38 pass. The two that fail are
`s13_1` and `s13_2`, by 1e-5 to 2e-4 relative. Those learning loops move
the cutoff of `fi.lowpass(1)`/`fi.highpass(1)` at every step. For a
time-varying filter, the two realizations differ, unlike at a fixed
setting: with a cutoff of `1000 + 500*os.osc(3)` the outputs differ by
4.7e-5, while at a fixed 1000 Hz they agree to 3.9e-16. The `fad` tangent
at a fixed setting agrees to 4e-19. These figures are to be remeasured if
the branch merges.

## Constant folding: a comparison of floats is an integer, as in C++

Commit date: 2026-10-06

Source commit: this commit

**Symptom.** On the faustlibraries branch `jos-300-fixes`, `os.phasor` and
`os.sawtooth` failed with FRS-EVAL-0099, "no case rule matches arguments",
while Faust C++ 2.90.3 compiles them. The branch dispatches on the precision
with a case:

```faust
_phasor_imp = _phasor_by_precision(ma.EPSILON > 1e-10)
with { _phasor_by_precision(0) = _phasor_flt; _phasor_by_precision(1) = _phasor_fix; };
```

`saw2ptr` and `sawN` have the same form. Minimal reproducer:
`process = f(1.5 > 1.0) with { f(0) = 10; f(1) = 20; };`.

**Cause.** `fold_binop` in `crates/normalize/src/simplify.rs` folded a
comparison with a float operand to `sigReal(1.0)`. The argument simplified
by `simplify_pattern` was then `boxReal(1.0)`, which is not the tree of the
pattern `1`. C++ `gtNode` ... `neNode` in `compiler/tlib/node.hh` return a
`bool`, which selects `Node(int)`, so C++ gets `int(1)`. The bitwise
operations had the same defect: C++ `andNode`, `orNode` and `xorNode` are
`int(x) op int(y)`, while faust-rs gave a real. Both are now integers.
Shifts on floats still do not fold, as before.

**Validation.**
- New tests:
  - `simplify_float_comparison_and_bitwise_are_int_like_cpp` in normalize;
  - `eval_process_matches_a_float_comparison_against_an_integer_rule_like_cpp`
    in `crates/eval/tests/core_eval.rs`, five sources compared with the
    C++ choice.
- Against C++ on `jos-300-fixes`, rendered sample by sample over 100 032
  samples (`faust -lang c` against faustprobe), the outputs agree to 1 ulp
  in both precisions:
  - `os.phasor(1,440)`;
  - `os.sawtooth(440)` and `os.sawtooth(2)`;
  - `fi.lowpass(1, …)` and `fi.highpass(1, …)` with a modulated cutoff.
- Gates:
  - fmt, `clippy -D warnings`, golden-check and the five structure gates
    are green;
  - the workspace tests are green against faustlibraries `master`;
  - compile-budget cannot run on this machine. The change adds one `match`
    on a constant pair.

**Still open on `jos-300-fixes`.** Three tutorial tests stay outside their
margins, the cause being library numerics and not faust-rs:
- `s05_2` reads 1216 Hz for 1206;
- `s13_2` reads 2574.73 for 2574.69;
- `s13_1` reads `drive` 20.945 for 20.94.

The branch rewrites `tf1s`, which `fi.lowpass(1)` and `fi.highpass(1)` use,
as a trapezoidal (TPT) section: the same transfer function, a different
trajectory under modulation. faust-rs follows C++ on that filter, as
measured above. If the branch merges, the tutorial figures have to be
remeasured.
