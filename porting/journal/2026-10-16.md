# 2026-10-16

## eval: smaller eval-cache entries, and the cache's stores pinned

Commit date: 2026-10-16

Source commit: this commit (parent `13040c0`).

After the `gApplyMemo` port, what remained of the coopiano voice's
evaluation was the `(expr, env)` eval cache (`porting/MEMOIZATION.md`
§2.4b): 10.05 M entries of 128 bytes, its rehash from 2^23 to 2^24 buckets
being the 4.3 GB transient peak at the end of evaluation. An entry is the
32-byte key and an `EvalValue`, which was 96 bytes because
`EvalValue::PatternMatcher` held its `PatternMatcherValue` inline, although
the cache only ever stores boxes and closures.

- `crates/eval/src/environment.rs`: the variant is
  `PatternMatcher(Box<PatternMatcherValue>)`. `EvalValue` is 40 bytes (the
  32-byte `ClosureValue` and a tag), an entry 72. The two functions that
  advance a matcher (`apply_pattern_matcher_value`,
  `lower_pattern_matcher_to_symbolic`) take the box and re-wrap it without
  reallocating; `LoopDetector::store_pm` / `get_pm` move in and out of the
  dense store. Mapping status unchanged (`adapted`, a host-side
  representation).
- `crates/eval/src/loop_detector.rs`: `eval_cache_insert` pins the store of
  every eval-cache key in `pinned_environments`, as `apply_memo_insert`
  already did, closing the open question of §2.4e: an `EnvFrameKey` holds raw
  store and source-context pointers, and a store freed during the pass (an
  `Environment::empty()` root of `a2sb`) could hand its address to a new one
  whose layers would hit stale entries. `last_pinned` makes the insertion
  path one pointer comparison unless the store changes. The
  `FAUST_EVAL_APPLY_PROFILE` line now also prints the number of pinned
  stores (9 on the voice, 76 360 layers, 3 more than the apply memo alone
  pinned).
- Measured on the voice (`dsp/coopiano_hammer.dsp -double -I .`, release
  binaries before and after, five interleaved runs, `/usr/bin/time -l`):
  evaluation 2.14 s to 1.80 s best of five (medians 2.27 s to 1.84 s);
  maximum resident set 4.51 GB to 2.83 GB; peak memory footprint 4.04 GB to
  2.47 GB; whole compilation 12.5 s to 12.2 s; generated C++ byte-identical.
  The maximum resident set is now reached at the end of the compilation, not
  in evaluation: the freed table's pages stay resident, while the live
  footprint after evaluation is 0.5 GB to 0.6 GB in both builds. The pin
  itself is within noise (boxed variant without it: 1.78 s best, 1.93 s
  median).
- Compile-budget basket by hand (release binaries before and after, best of
  five, `faust-rs` CLI): every case within 0.99 to 1.03, the worst a 2.2 s
  vector case (`spectral_level`). The gate itself refuses on this machine,
  its calibration DSP running under the 4 ms floor.
- Tests: `an_eval_value_is_a_closure_and_a_tag` (`environment.rs`, the size
  bound) and `a_store_named_by_a_memo_key_outlives_its_environment`
  (`loop_detector.rs`, two roots evaluated with one detector, the first
  dropped; rejects the pin removed from `eval_cache_insert`).
  `porting/MEMOIZATION.md` §2.4b and §2.4e updated.
- Validation: fmt, clippy `-D warnings`, the workspace tests (3224 passed),
  the five structure gates and `golden-check` (292 cases) pass;
  `compile-budget-check` refuses as before (calibration `karplus.dsp` 3 ms).

## eval: pattern matchers are persistent values (`DIFF-GAP-018` closed)

Commit date: 2026-10-16

Source commit: this commit (parent `d854350`).

A `case` function passed as an argument and applied more than once to
different arguments kept the pattern-variable bindings of its first
application: `h(p) = p(2), p(3); process = h(case { (x) => x + 1; });` failed
with `FRS-EVAL-0099` (C++: `3, 4`), and with a later rule binding no
variable, `f = case { (x) => x + 1; (3) => 100; }; process = h(f), f(3);`
silently gave `3, 100, 4` (C++: `3, 4, 4`). `apply_pattern_matcher` bound
the variables in place into the matcher's rule environments, which every
copy of the matcher shares (a `boxPatternMatcher` handle, a matcher bound to
a parameter); C++ pushes a new layer per binding
(`E[r->r] = pushValueDef(assoc->id, Z1, E[r->r])`,
`compiler/patternmatcher/patternmatcher.cpp`).

- `crates/eval/src/pattern_matcher.rs`: a binding pushes a child layer and
  replaces the rule's slot with it, one layer per rule and argument rather
  than per binding (equivalent: the nonlinearity lookup reads the new layer
  first and still stops at the rule's barrier). Mapping `1:1` in behaviour.
- `crates/eval/src/lib.rs`, `eval_case_value`: one barrier layer for every
  rule, as C++ `listn(len(rules), pushEnvBarrier(env))`; nothing writes into
  it any more.
- The apply memo's restriction to unshared matchers is lifted:
  `PatternMatcherValue::fresh_env` is no longer cleared on a stored handle
  or on a read from an environment, a copy of a matcher in its initial state
  being the same function as the fresh one. Measured to matter: no change on
  the 134 DSPs of `tests/impulse-tests/dsp` and `tests/compile-budget/dsp`
  nor on the coopiano voice, but a `case` passed to itself through an
  overlapping recursion, `fibk(k, n) = k(k, n-1) + k(k, n-2)`, which only
  evaluates since the fix, goes from exponential (release, `n = 27` 2.2 s;
  the reference `8eebea429`, without `gApplyMemo`, 50.8 s at `n = 25`) to
  linear (C++ 2.90.8 likewise). `porting/MEMOIZATION.md` §2.4e and §2.4b
  updated; the eval cache still does not keep matchers.
- Cost (release binaries before and after, best of five): compile-budget
  basket by hand within noise (worst 1.05 on an 11 ms case; the gate itself
  refuses on this machine, calibration under its 4 ms floor); coopiano voice
  identical memo counters, time and maximum resident set, generated C++
  byte-identical, 76 392 environment layers before and 76 357 after; over
  the 134 corpus DSPs 30 751 layers before and 38 186 after, with the same
  eval-cache entries.
- Tests (`crates/eval/src/apply.rs`):
  `a_shared_matcher_does_not_answer_for_a_fresh_one` now asserts `p(3) = 4`;
  `a_shared_matcher_keeps_its_applications_apart` (five programs, values
  from C++ `8eebea429` and 2.90.8: a `case` passed as an argument, nonlinear
  rules, a partially applied shared matcher, an outer definition named like
  a pattern variable); `a_case_handle_passed_through_a_recursion_is_memoized`
  (`fibk(fibk, 40)` under the 60 s budget); and the structural
  `applying_a_matcher_leaves_its_rule_environments_untouched`. Mutations
  rejected: in-place binding (four tests fail, `f(3)` gives 100), and
  `fresh_env` cleared on a stored handle (`fibk` times out).
- `DIFF-GAP-018` removed from `porting/faust-rs-vs-faust-cpp-differences-en.md`
  §9.

## eval: port of the C++ application memo (`gApplyMemo`)

Commit date: 2026-10-16

Source commit: this commit (parent `bf65d31`).

A call inside the body of a lambda was evaluated again at every application of
the lambda, so `f_i(m) = f_{i-1}(m) : \(x).(x + 0.5 * f_{i-1}(m))` cost `2^D`,
and a `case` recursion with overlapping calls (`fib`, `ma.chebychev`) grew like
Fibonacci. Both were found porting the coopiano piano to Faust
(`faust-coopiano/faust-rs-issues/RAPPORT.md`, items 1, 2 and 13), whose voice
spent 12.8 s and 11 GB of resident memory in evaluation. The cause: every
application evaluates its body in a new environment layer, which the
`(expr, env)` eval cache has never seen. Upstream Faust fixed the same cost
with `gApplyMemo` (`6789c5da8`, #1290), absent from the reference branch
`8eebea429`.

- `crates/eval/src/apply.rs`: `beta_reduce` memoizes one beta-reduction of an
  abstraction closure, keyed by the `abstr`, the identity of the captured
  environment and the evaluated argument; the head of
  `apply_pattern_matcher_value` memoizes a fresh matcher applied to its full
  argument list, keyed by the `case`, the environment it was evaluated in and
  the arguments. Only successful box and closure results are stored. Mapping
  `adapted` (C++ keys on the hash-consed closure or `boxPatternMatcher` tree and
  the whole argument list): rationale, the treatment of box functions and the
  lifetime are in `porting/MEMOIZATION.md` §2.4e.
- A matcher is fresh (`PatternMatcherValue::fresh_env`) until it is stored as a
  `boxPatternMatcher` handle or read from an environment. A shared matcher
  keeps the bindings of its earlier applications, because pattern variables
  are bound in place into its rule environments; that defect predates the memo
  and is recorded as `DIFF-GAP-018`; the memo is kept off shared matchers so it
  does not spread it.
- `LoopDetector::pinned_environments` keeps alive every environment store an
  apply-memo key points to, so its address cannot be reused within the pass.
- The automaton cache holds `Rc<Automaton>`, shared with every matcher,
  instead of a deep copy per `case` evaluation, lookup and handle read.
- `FAUST_EVAL_APPLY_PROFILE=1` prints the memo's probes and hits per kind and
  the sizes of the memo, of the eval cache and of the environment store.
- Measured (release, Apple M5): `r01_eval_call_reuse.sh` lambda column 67 ms
  to 0.10 ms at `D = 14`; `fib(22)` 194 ms to 0.19 ms; the coopiano voice
  evaluation 12.8 s to 2.0 s, maximum resident set 11.1 GB to 4.5 GB, whole
  compilation 22.9 s to 12.1 s, generated C++ byte-identical. Three
  compilations of the voice at once now take 14.8 s each with 0.6 s of system
  time (`faust-rs` to C++; the report measured 216 s each and 126 s of system
  time through `faustprobe` before). The rest of the
  voice's evaluation is the eval cache itself, 10 M entries of about
  128 bytes.
- Documentation corrected: `MEMOIZATION.md` §2.4c and §3.7 said C++
  hash-conses its environments. Its layers are `unique` (`pushNewLayer`); its
  cross-level hits come from `evalIdDef`, which evaluates a bound value under
  the empty environment, and from `gApplyMemo`. §3.7 is reframed as bound
  values evaluated under a canonical environment.
- Tests: four in `crates/eval/src/apply.rs`, each under a 60 s budget, with
  five mutations rejected (memo off for abstractions or for matchers, the
  environment dropped from either key, a stored handle kept fresh).
- Compile-budget basket, release binaries before and after, best of five: no
  case slower beyond noise (worst 1.03 on an 8 ms case); `dx7_alg5` `--check`
  124 ms to 76 ms, `reverb_designer` 186 ms to 136 ms. The
  `compile-budget-check` gate itself refuses on this machine (calibration
  `karplus.dsp` 3 ms, under its 4 ms floor), as it did before this change; the
  comparison was done by hand for that reason.
- Validation: fmt, clippy `-D warnings`, the workspace tests (3219 passed),
  the five structure gates and `golden-check` (292 cases) pass.
