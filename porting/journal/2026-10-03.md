## Proposal: two teaching examples for `adaptive_fad`

Commit date: 2026-10-03

Source commit: this commit

`porting/adaptive-fad-teaching-examples-proposal-2026-10-03-en.md` proposes
how to teach `optimizers.lib`'s `adaptive_fad` / `adaptive_rad` (a whole
program learning its own sliders through `cinputs`, `cinput` and the `"*"`
modulation), which neither the tutorial nor the DDSP example set covers yet.
Every figure in it was measured with `faustprobe` on `control-inputs-wildcard`
rebased on `main-dev`.

- Simple: a new tutorial §13 on a two-slider program (low-pass cutoff and
  gain), in three programs: reading the controls with `cinput`; one Adam rate
  for both, where the cutoff barely moves and the gain overshoots to 1.63;
  one rate per slider read from its range, which reaches 2500 Hz and 1.2 with
  a residual of 2.4e-8 rms.
- Ambitious: DDSP example 15, an ordinary six-slider drive pedal that learns
  its hidden setting from the same program under named literal modulations.
  All six sliders come within 1e-5 in 200 000 samples at 126x real time;
  `adaptive_rad` drifts on it, since the four filters are recursive.
- Findings: `fi.peak_eq` is not differentiable at its 0 dB default (`abs` of
  the gain), so the slider jumps to its bound; `rad` refuses the read-only
  table of `os.osc` (FRS-SFIR-0004 in the block reverse sweep), contrary to
  `docs/rad-note-en.md` §3.6, which is being fixed next.

The documentation, the fixture and the tests are not part of this commit;
the proposal lists their deliverables and pass criteria.
